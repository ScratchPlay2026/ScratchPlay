Chapter 2: How to Start Your Game
On the BIOS screen, press U to upload a game, & you're all set! This is meant for IFrames for scratch projects.
Here's a step by step tutorial on how to upload a Scratch game.
Step 1: Go to the scratch project page.
Step 2: Click Copy Link.
Step 3: Click Copy embed

Chapter 3: Game Keys.
These are the 9 mandatory keys needed to play games. You'll need these: ←, →, ↑, ↓, ⎵ (space bar), Z, X, C, V.
